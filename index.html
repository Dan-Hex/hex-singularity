Get to know me

About Me

Hi! I'm Dan John Cleofe, an Information Technology student from Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela. I am passionate about creating Graphic Designs and Web Development, while exploring the field of Information Technology. I enjoy exploring different aspects of design and development, while building skills that can contribute to meaningful solutions.

My background spans Visual Design, Web wireframes, and artistic expression towards different mediums in both graphic design and website making. Although I am still developing my skills, I am committed to continuous learning and improvement.

My goal is to create more Graphical Designs to express my thoughts and ideas, as well as showcasing my skills in making artistic outputs. To improve my skills in creating impactful wireframes to develop websites that serves better user experience and ambiance, and to explore more designs online. I am eager to learn more and create more design outputs that can be appreciated by others, and aim to deliver a more satisfying experience in the field of design and web development.
